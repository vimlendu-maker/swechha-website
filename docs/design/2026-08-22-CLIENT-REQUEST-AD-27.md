## Two ambiguities and how this pass reads them

**"Keep Mission and Vision, delete"** — the sentence is unfinished. Read as:
*keep* the Mission and Vision block; the trailing "delete" attaches to the next
bullet (the Boring-and-unnecessary section), which is the only thing named after
it. Nothing else on `/about` is deleted on the strength of an unfinished
sentence. Recorded as a ruling so it can be corrected in one line.

**"May be follow him page impact band style"** — read as *the /impact page's
impact-band style*. That page's band is the reference for every project's
impact figures.
