## Brand language — non-negotiable

- **Type**: Fraunces for section headings / promise lines / closing lines; Archivo
  (condensed caps, wide tracking) for wordmarks, card titles, micro-labels, numerals;
  Instrument Sans for body. Two earlier hierarchies were rejected — do not re-litigate.
- **Canvas** black/charcoal; **mustard #E1A32B is the primary accent**; colour is
  SIGNAL, never decoration. **Red = a broken limit. Green = what has been recovered
  (closed list).** One hue live per band. Never red on a control. State is carried by
  SHAPE as well as hue.
- Selective colour is OFF site-wide. Frames that arrive with colour baked in take no
  filter class.
- No build step: `home.html` is self-contained and carries its own `<style>`. There is
  no shared stylesheet — a "fix it once" instinct is wrong on this codebase.
- No reveal/IntersectionObserver system exists in this file. Do not introduce one.
