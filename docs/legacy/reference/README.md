## `european-commission-marine-pollution.jpg`

A **European Commission** (Directorate-General for Maritime Affairs and
Fisheries) infographic — "MARINE POLLUTION: Cleaning up the oceans to protect
ecosystems, wildlife and our health". It carries the EU flag and the
Commission's own mark, and no GIZ, German ministry or Swechha mark anywhere.

It was committed to `public/images/giz-marine/marine-pollution.jpg` in
`0176d7f` alongside the seven GIZ poster JPEGs, and the content audit
(`docs/design/2026-08-24-CONTENT-COMPLETENESS-AUDIT.md`, item M-04) counted it
as an eighth Swechha asset. It is not one. AD-26 §3 records where it came
from: it sat *one level up* from `ALL POSTERS FINAL - JPEG/` in the source
archive, i.e. it was a reference download filed beside the real work and got
swept in with it.

Publishing it in a Swechha poster gallery would have misattributed EU material
to this organisation. Removed from `public/` on 25 August 2026 (AD-42); the
real set is the ten posters rendered from the project's own PDF into
`public/images/posters/giz-marine/`.
