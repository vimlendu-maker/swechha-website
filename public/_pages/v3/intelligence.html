Environmental intelligence dashboards — nearly real time

Every situation
we read

A situation is one measurement, against one published limit, with the gap named. Not a score, not a mood, not a colour. Where nobody publishes a limit, none is invented.

6

situations

Air, the Yamuna, heat, forest fire, forest loss, extreme rain.

6

different units

An index, mg/L, degrees, km², hectares, days. There is no exchange rate between them.

6

kinds of limit

A ceiling. A floor. An absolute. A requirement naming no quantity. And, once, none at all.

There is no total, and there never will be. No two of the 6 share a kind of limit, and 6 units do not average. One figure for “the environment” decides something on your behalf without saying what.

The four state words

LIVEthe value can change between one reading and the next PERIODICdelivered on a cadence — a yearly table, a monthly sample OUT OF SEASONthe window is shut; the record still stands DEMO DATAa specimen, and there are none in the set below

The word describes how the source delivers. Yamuna reads PERIODIC because CPCB publishes once a year; Air reads LIVE because it sits in front of an hourly feed. Every card names the period its own reading covers.

The six

One card each, and the kind of limit each reading is held to.

Air Delhi 228 air quality index Poor · 14:00 IST a ceiling AQI 100 CPCB, National Air Quality Index 2.3× the limit The only reading on this site that can change while you look at it. LIVE Yamuna Delhi 0.3 mg/L dissolved oxygen at or below the detection limit · CPCB 2025 a floor > 5.0 mg/L Primary Water Quality Criteria, E(P) Rules 1986 no measurable oxygen The one number here that has to be read downwards. Lower is worse, and it is at the bottom. PERIODIC Heat India 48.3 °C Jodhpur, 2024 · the record in this archive an absolute 47 °C IMD, severe heat wave 1.3 °C above it The season is shut until 1 March 2027. The record is true either way. OUT OF SEASON Forest fire India 34,562 km² burnt in one season November 2023 to June 2024 none No legal threshold. no statute publishes one nothing to be over The only situation here with no threshold to break. That is not reassurance. PERIODIC Forest loss India 2.43 million hectares of tree cover 2001–2025 · satellite-measured a requirement approval, not a quantity Forest (Conservation) Act, 1980 India’s own report says +156.4 km² Two official sources, opposite directions. Neither is lying. PERIODIC Climate event India 13 days over the heavy-rain threshold Mangaluru, 2025 a class, crossed 64.5 mm in 24 hours IMD rainfall day categories crossed 13 times in one year Not one breach. A count of them. PERIODIC

Read the middle line of each card. 4 of the 6 are over a published limit. One is below a published minimum, which is the same failure pointing the other way. One is measured against a legal requirement that names no quantity at all. And one has no threshold in law to break — which is the weakest position of the six, not the safest.

What we do about it

Three campaigns. None of them is a solution to a national number, and saying otherwise would be the same trick as a single score.

We for Yamuna

since 2000

The founding campaign, and still the organisation’s spine. It is the reason there is a Yamuna page at all, and the reason the walk exists.

The reading it works against

Monsoon Wooding

annual

Roughly 5,000 trees a year across Delhi NCR, and over 50,000 planted and survived in total. Survived is the organisation’s own word for it, and it is the honest one — planting is not the same measurement as living.

The record

Delhi I Can’t See You

One of the three current campaigns.

What we work on

The gap between a campaign and a national figure is the honest subject. Fifty thousand surviving trees is a real number and it is not a reply to 2.43 million hectares. Both are on this site, in their own units, and neither is used to cancel the other.

Every reading here is public, dated and linked to the document it came from.
