Publications

Two we
printed.

Not a catalogue. One book about a neighbourhood we worked in for years, and one guide written to be carried into a shop. Both open whole, free, with no address asked for.

The publications

Each one is the reading copy. Nothing is behind a form, and nothing asks who you are.

Book · 2015

Khirki Extension, Delhi

A record of one Delhi neighbourhood — its water, its waste, its building, and the people who explained all three to us. Made over the years Swechha worked there, and printed rather than posted because the people in it wanted something they could hold.

Read the book PDF, 10 MB

Guide · 2010

The Sustainable Shopping Basket

A lifestyle and shopping guide, made with GIZ. Written to answer the question that follows every talk about consumption — what do I actually buy instead — and short enough to be read in a queue.

Read the guide PDF, 2.4 MB

What is not here

Recorded so that nobody looks twice.

The This Girl Can book was asked for and then withdrawn. No book file was found for it either — what exists is a poster series and a set of photographs, and neither is a publication.

The GIZ marine-plastic infographics exist and are not on this page. They belong with the campaign they were made for, and that campaign does not have a page yet.
