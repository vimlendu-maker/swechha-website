What it sets out to do

The objectives, as we would state them to a school or a funder — not the outcomes, which are three bands down.

Plant so that something arrives

The test of a butterfly park is whether an insect nobody invited turns up. It is a slow test and it cannot be staged for a camera.

Be wanted after the money stops

A garden lasts because somebody needs what is in it. The aim is a plot a neighbourhood would defend, since the alternative ending is tarmac.

Depth on one site, not a layer on fifty

On airshed work the intent is a decade in a single park with the green cover measured at each end. It is the only version of this that anybody can check.

A park nobody needs gets paved.

Which is why usefulness is the maintenance plan and not a nice extra.

Strategy and activities

The method, then the named things that actually happen. Choose one.

It is always somebody else's land

These are public parks and institutional grounds, not our sites. Which means every one of them needed a permission before it needed a plant.

Butterfly parks

You do not build one, you plant one: host plants for the caterpillars, nectar plants for the adults, and then you leave it alone long enough for something to find it. Over seventy of them, across Delhi NCR.

A butterfly, in one of our own frames. Not a photograph of one of the parks — we hold none.

Herb gardens

The same trick, aimed at people. Over twenty of them, planted where somebody will actually pick from them. Usefulness is what keeps a garden alive after the funding ends.

A cultivated bed at our farm, not one of these gardens.

Airshed parks

Ten years is the honest unit for this kind of work, and ten years is why almost nobody does it. Vasant Kunj is the park we have taken all the way, and the span it moved through is drawn at the top of this page.

A restored city waterway of ours. The Vasant Kunj park is photographed in neither state.

Who it is for

Named, not described as a category. Where a group is on this list because they asked for it rather than because we chose them, it says so.

Whoever already walks through the park

These are public parks, so the people this is for are the ones who were going to be there anyway. Nobody is enrolled in a butterfly park.

The insects

Said plainly, because it is the design brief. A butterfly park is built to be found by something that was never invited.

The institutions holding the land

Schools, offices and civic bodies with ground they are not using. What they get back needs less water and less mowing than lawn.

The people who will pick from it

Herb gardens are planted for use. If nobody harvests it, the garden has failed on its own terms rather than on ours.

Impact

What it has done, what that figure counts, and what it cannot yet say.

Over seventy parks, over twenty gardens

Across Delhi NCR. PVR, American Express and Adobe behind the butterfly parks; Amazon and American Express behind the herb gardens.

One park, five per cent to ninety

The Vasant Kunj airshed park, over a decade of work, with The American Embassy behind it. It is one park. It is not a city figure and it is not an organisation figure, and we will not set it as either.
