The one that is coming, not the one that came

Air kills more. The river is worse. But heat is the only situation on this site whose death toll more than doubled in a single year — and it is the one nobody can move away from, insulate against, or filter.

A heatwave has no plume, no smell and no photograph. It leaves no damage to survey. It kills indoors, at night, in people who were already ill, and it is recorded as something else.

8 of these 14 cities set their all-time record in 2024. Not spread across thirty-five years. One year.

Lightning kills more than everything else combined — 2,825 deaths, and almost never a story. And 1,626 deaths (20.6%) have no named cause at all.

Nobody publishes heat-related hospital admissions, lost work or school days missed in India. Those are the numbers a city needs to plan with, and they do not exist publicly.

What counts as heat

What counts as heat

India does not define a heatwave by a temperature. It defines it by a distance from normal — and the temperature it starts from depends on where you are.

A day is a heat wave when the maximum clears its local floor and stands 4.5°C above the normal for that place and date. 6.5°C above makes it severe.

40°C plains
37°C coastal
30°C hills

The floor by zone. Applying the plains figure to Chennai would under-count every coastal city on this page.

There is also an absolute rule, with no reference to normal: 45°C anywhere is a heat wave, 47°C is severe.

So the same 41°C afternoon is a heatwave in March and an ordinary day in June. That is the point, not a loophole. A body acclimatised to June heat is not the body that meets it in March.

A departure needs a normal. Each city gets its own, computed from 1991-2020: for every calendar day, the mean maximum over a ±7-day window. About 450 samples per day.

A departure is meaningless against somebody else's normal. Chennai's normal and Jodhpur's are 10 C apart.

Reproducible, not quoted. Because the normal comes from the same archive as the readings, you can recompute every departure on this page from the source. It is not IMD’s published departure and does not claim to be.

IMD declares for a subdivision on two or more stations. These are single grid points, so these are days MEETING the temperature criteria at each location, not IMD declarations.

So every figure here is days meeting IMD’s criteria at this location, never heatwave days declared by IMD. Different quantities. The second is not ours to publish.

And it is modelled. ERA5 reanalysis — a model constrained by observations, not a station thermometer. An IMD station series would outrank this, not confirm it. Reanalysis also smooths extremes hardest at the tail this subject lives in, and contains no urban heat island.

A grid point is not a city. Fourteen grid points are not a country.

Sources. Criteria: India Meteorological Department — heat wave criteria. Readings: Open-Meteo ERA5 archive. Deaths: Accidental Deaths & Suicides in India 2024, Table 1.0 — Number and Share of Accidental Deaths due to Forces of Nature and Other Causes, 2023 & 2024.

What is rising

What is rising

Not the afternoons. The nights.

Five measures, the first eighteen seasons against the last, counted across all 14 cities. The pale bar is cities where it rose, the grey where it did not move, the dark where it fell.

Dry-bulb heat is not the measure that is rising. Humid heat and warm nights are. Across these 14 stations, the number that moves most consistently upward is not the hottest afternoon — it is the number of nights that never fall below 28°C, and the number of days whose apparent temperature clears 45°C. Peak daytime maxima are close to flat at most stations and fall at some.

Counted as cities, not averaged. How many of the 14 stations move each way between the first and second halves of the record. Published as a count of stations rather than a national average, because averaging 14 grid points does not produce a national trend.

Why the afternoons are the wrong thing to watch
  1. It is not evidence that heat is not intensifying. Three of the four things that make heat dangerous are not dry-bulb maximum: humidity, night-time recovery, and duration.
  2. A reanalysis smooths local extremes hardest at exactly the tail this metric lives in, and contains no urban heat island for cities that have doubled their built area.
  3. A grid point is not a city, and 14 grid points are not a country.
  4. IMD's own declarations are made on station data across subdivisions and are a different quantity from this one.

And who is looking

Lighter bars are months inside the season. Peak 78,038 in Jun 2024, floor 5,174 in Feb 2026 — 15.1×. Attention peaks with the season and is gone by August. NCRB publishes the death count the following year, when nobody is looking.

Who is watching

Who is watching

India runs a daily satellite heat outlook. Its layer list makes the same argument this page does.

Temperature Based Heat daily maximum temperature
Moisture and Temperature Based Heatincludes humidity temperature with humidity — what a body experiences
Temperature Based Heat, deviation from climatology the departure, which is what IMD's own heatwave criteria are built on
Moisture and Temperature Based Heat, deviation from climatologyincludes humidity both at once

India's own heat product does not measure heat with temperature alone. Two of its four layers add moisture. Two express the answer as a departure from normal. That is the official instrument agreeing, in its design, with the two things this page had to compute for itself.

What you can do

What you can do

Heat kills the people who cannot leave it.

The season starts 1 March

Not May, when the headlines arrive. Construction and delivery workers, street vendors, anyone whose room does not cool at night — they are exposed from the first week.

India’s heat outlook

Ask for the plan

Cities write Heat Action Plans. Ask if yours has one, whether it names shaded spaces and water points near your ward, and whether outdoor workers were consulted. A plan nobody can find is not a plan.

The work against this reading

Eco Action builds the small green infrastructure a city actually has room for — over seventy butterfly parks and over twenty herb gardens across Delhi NCR. In one Vasant Kunj park, green cover went from 5% to 90% over a decade.

And why it is not a reply

That is one park, and heat is regional. Shade cools the body standing under it and nothing past that: it does not lower the 48.3°C in this archive and it did not prevent the 1,832 deaths recorded in 2024. A canopy is a real intervention at the scale of a bus stop. The reading is at the scale of a country.
