What a situation
page carries

Three parts, in this order. Air and the Yamuna are built. The rest inherit the same template as their data lands.

The reading

The value, its unit, the band it falls in, the legal limit it is judged against and the multiplier. Plus the source, the hour and the cadence.

The context

The national or system-wide dataset the local reading sits inside, as a persistent side panel. Explainers for every measure. What is measured and what is modelled.

The record

Every past reading at its own address, the orders and policy that concern this situation, and what you can do about it.
