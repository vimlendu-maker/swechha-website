The full instrument

All situations  /  Air  /  07:00 IST, 19 August 2026

A monitor reading is not an exposureThe index describes the air at one instrument at one hour. What a person actually breathes depends on where they are, how long they are outside and how high off the road they sleep. This page reports the instrument, and says so, rather than implying it has measured you.

Everything below is either measured or modelled, and the two are set differently on purpose. If it is on a solid rule in the condensed face, an instrument recorded it. If it is italic on a dashed rule, a model produced it and you should weigh it accordingly.

What is
actually being
measured?

Every measure on this site carries an explainer beside it. A number without one is a number for analysts, and this is not a site for analysts. These do not change from day to day, which is exactly why they are worth writing well once.

AQI

One number for six poisons

The air quality index folds six pollutants into a single 0 to 500 figure and reports whichever is worst. It is not a concentration. It is a rank, and a rank hides which pollutant is doing the damage.

412 today. The safe limit is 100 and the WHO would call 100 unsafe.

PM2.5

Small enough to enter blood

Particles under two and a half microns. They pass the lung wall and enter the bloodstream, which is why they are measured separately from everything else in the air.

218 micrograms per cubic metre today. The Indian daily standard is 60.

PM10

Dust you can feel

Coarser particles from roads, construction and soil. They stop in the upper airway, which is why they make you cough and why they respond to water spraying when finer particles do not.

392 micrograms per cubic metre today. The Indian daily standard is 100.

A note on CPCB station data

The station network grew from six monitors in 2001 to thirty-six today, so an early year is measured by fewer instruments in fewer places than a recent one. A rise in the count can therefore be a rise in coverage rather than a rise in pollution, and the first decade of this chart should be read as indicative.

Swechha does not adjust for this, because any adjustment would be a model and would have to be marked as one. The unadjusted count is published with its caveat instead.

What happens
next?

Swechha does not forecast air quality and is not going to pretend to. The slot is designed, and until a forecast source is wired it shows what it actually has, which is nothing.

No forecast source
