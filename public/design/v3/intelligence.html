What is in
season?

Every situation carries a validity window, and the window is what puts it in the list. Heat runs March to mid-July. Monsoon runs June to September. Air, the river and forest loss are permanent. Nobody has to remember to switch anything on in March.

Two shapes of window, and they behave differently. A recurring season repeats every year and says when it returns. A one-off window has a real end and never comes back.

Why this page
goes red

Three colours are allowed on this site and each one does one job. Nothing is ever coloured because it looked better that way.

Mustard

A human act, and therefore the interactive colour. Buttons, links, hover, focus, the Give chip, the filters above. It never touches a reading and it never means anything is fine.

Red

A published legal limit has been broken. Applied by comparing the reading to the limit, never by a designer. The rail, the word, the top band, the multiplier. Never a button, never a link, never the logo.

Green

What came back. Tonnes out of the river, gardens planted, saplings standing. Green only ever labels a past-tense recovery, which is why it can sit next to a number without being read as a forecast.

Feed state is a separate question from severity and uses shape, not colour: a filled square is live, a hollow one is periodic, a hatched one is demo data, a dashed one is out of season. A situation can be in breach and be demo data at the same time.
