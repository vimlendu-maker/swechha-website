Our work

Projects

Long-term work.
Rooted in place.

Projects are Swechha’s sustained programmes — the work we keep doing with schools, farms, young people and communities across India.

Five projects.
One direction.

Each project has its own place, its own partners and its own people, but they run on the same conviction: that change holds when it is built with a community rather than delivered to one.

Programmes, not campaigns. Measured in years.

A project page carries what it did, where, with whom, and what changed — alongside the stories, films and knowledge that came out of it. Campaigns move faster and journeys take you there; projects are the long arc.

Journeys and campaigns are listed separately under Our Work.
