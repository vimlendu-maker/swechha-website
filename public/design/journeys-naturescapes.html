Naturescapes

School journeys into India’s natural worlds.

Naturescapes is our school journeys programme, ranging from two to five days, to a range of ecological destinations. These journeys bring classrooms closer to nature — where learning is lived, connections are made, and responsibility grows.

Journeys that bring
learning to life.

From forests and mountains to deserts and coasts, our journeys are designed to deepen ecological understanding, encourage empathy and inspire action.

Not just a trip. A transformative experience.
