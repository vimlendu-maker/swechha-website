Option A

Editorial hero, then NOW as a tall section — with a live ticker above.

The hero carries brand only. Every piece of intelligence lives in one place below it, exactly as Environmental Intelligence §31 draws the component: dominant situation and signal row inside a single SWECHHA NOW block.

  • The ticker is a pointer, not a panel — one line, so liveness still lands above the fold without competing with the headline.
  • Air is the dominant reading, so the signal row carries the other four themes. Nothing duplicated, nothing orphaned.
  • Hero is 76vh and NOW is 72vh, so the two read as sequence rather than as two homepages.
  • The hero never degrades on a quiet news day, because it holds no live value.

Option C

Two stacked full-bleed banners — no ticker.

Both get a whole screen. The sequence reads as two statements: who we are, then what is happening right now. Same content as A, different weighting.

  • No ticker, so the first screen is pure brand — the calmest possible opening.
  • NOW is deliberately not photo-led. It is dark and data-led, so two full-viewport banners contrast rather than compete — this is the fix for the "two homepages" risk.
  • The numeral goes much larger, because it now owns a whole screen rather than sharing one.
  • Cost: the visitor must scroll past two full screens before reaching any of the work.
