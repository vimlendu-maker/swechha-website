Gram Anubhav

Live the village.
Learn for life.

Gram Anubhav is our rural immersion journeys programme, designed for school groups to experience village life firsthand. Stay, learn and work alongside communities and discover the wisdom of sustainable living.

Journeys that
stay with you.

Our 2–5 day rural immersion journeys invite students to step out of classrooms and into real life.
